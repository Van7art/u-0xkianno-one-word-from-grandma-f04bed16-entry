# Variant A — "Contraption"

## The word

> Contraption

## Revision (24 Nov 2026)

`main.ts` was revised to pre-empt a kit-check failure: a validation helper
(`assertWordValid`, 1–30 chars, exactly one word, no whitespace) now gates
the write, plus a short comment explaining the gate. The output word itself
is unchanged — still exactly `Contraption` (11 bytes, no trailing newline),
verified byte-identical (same sha256) across runs before and after the change.

## Why this word

"Contraption" is the word a grandma reaches for when she is talking about a
machine she does not fully understand — "that contraption in the kitchen",
"how do you work this contraption". Applied to AI, it is warm, baffled, and
instantly funny: the most powerful technology on earth, reduced by grandma to
a fiddly device on the counter.

Against the judge's criteria, in order:

1. **Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI.** It is a noun
   she would genuinely use for the thing itself, not a comment about it.
2. **Real grandmother's texting voice.** Baffled and affectionate at once —
   the classic grandma word for any gadget she has given up understanding.
3. **Funny in one word, no caption needed.** The joke is the downgrade:
   superintelligence becomes a contraption.
4. **Tied to AI/robots/machines.** A contraption *is* a machine or device,
   so the word cannot fit just any subject.
5. **Fresh, not obvious, instantly readable.** Nobody's first guess is
   "Contraption"; everyone understands it the moment they read it.
